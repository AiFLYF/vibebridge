## English

VibeBridge is not a medical device and is not a medical, psychological, or
clinical tool of any kind.

It does **not** diagnose, treat, assess severity, or measure improvement or
recovery. Everything it produces is descriptive statistics about the frequency
of observed activity during interaction, intended for personal long term
self reflection.

**No number, level, confidence value, or insight it generates constitutes a
clinical assessment, a medical diagnosis, or professional advice.** The
"levels" in a report describe which forms of activity are currently
comfortable, not a rating of ability. There is no passing threshold and they
should not be used to compare people.

This tool is not a substitute for professional support. If you or someone you
care about is in crisis, contact a qualified professional or your local
emergency services.
